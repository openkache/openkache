# OpenKache Community Guidelines

**English** | [한국어](./COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.ko.md)

OpenKache is a systems project focused on making caching less expensive and
easier to operate. The interesting work is in the hard edges: storage recovery,
safe operations, clear APIs, and documentation that helps people run it well.

If you are evaluating OpenKache, running it, asking a question, reporting a
problem, or sending a patch, you should get a clear answer and know what to do
next. We will be honest about what works, what does not, and what still needs
work.

If you want to contribute, start with the
[contribution guide](./CONTRIBUTING.md). These guidelines describe how we
expect people to treat one another while they do that work.

## How we work

Be direct and kind. Ask questions before assuming. Challenge designs and
measurements, not people. When reporting a problem, include the context needed
to reproduce it and say what is still uncertain. When proposing a change,
explain the tradeoffs and keep the scope small enough to review.

Make room for newcomers. A basic question can expose a confusing API or a
missing piece of documentation. A clear answer, a careful review, a
reproducible result, and an honest limitation are all valuable contributions.

Protect private information, credentials, and data. Give credit for ideas and
work. If new evidence changes your conclusion, update the discussion rather
than quietly leaving an old claim in place.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, stalking, or personal
  attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention, unwanted contact, or sexualized material.
- Doxxing or publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam, impersonation, deliberate disruption, or knowingly
  misleading claims.
- Unauthorized access, malicious code, or public disclosure of exploitable
  security details before maintainers have had a chance to respond.

## Reporting problems

Use a public issue for a reproducible bug, documentation gap, or feature idea.
Remove secrets and private data before posting.

Do not put vulnerability details or exploit instructions in a public issue.
No private reporting channel is currently published for this repository. Until
one is available, open an issue with only “security contact requested” and no
sensitive details, then wait for a maintainer to provide a private channel.

If you cannot safely describe a conduct concern in public, open a short issue
saying only “private conduct contact requested.” Do not include the incident
details.

## Moderation and scope

Maintainers may pause a thread, close an issue or pull request, remove content,
or block an account when behavior harms people or the project. We will explain
decisions when practical and prioritize safety, privacy, and keeping the
project usable.

These guidelines cover issues, pull requests, reviews, documentation, and
other channels used for OpenKache work. They are intended to make project
participation safer and more useful, not to regulate anyone’s private life.
